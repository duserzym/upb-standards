Scope: all 35 source records and all 51 material files listed in `library.toml`. Changes are confined to the source registry, material TOMLs and this report. No records were deleted. ## Method

Each of the 26 DOI-bearing records was requested with `curl -s https://api.crossref.org/works/<doi>`. All returned the intended work. Titles, author lists, journal or book, volume, issue, pagination and publication year were compared with Crossref and available paper front matter. No existing DOI pointed to a different paper. Older Wiley online dates were not substituted for print publication years. Missing issue numbers were not invented. All 27 literature displays were expanded; the eight non-literature descriptions were retained. Displays remain unique.

Local PDFs in `~/Github/literature/` were read without modification. Text was extracted with `pdftotext -layout` into `.audit-tmp/`; selected table pages were also rendered and inspected. The temporary folder was removed at the end. Eighteen cited PDFs were inspected. A “yes” in the PDF column means that the cited PDF was inspected, not that every associated field was verified. Exact checked blocks have a `locator`. Abstract-only checks say so explicitly.

The cited Schoene and Bowring (2006) paper was obtained from an [open repository](https://repository.geologyscience.ru/server/api/core/bitstreams/bce856b6-16e7-47d7-ad83-8213c8e63e75/content). Local Schoene2006a is the different GCA decay-constants paper. Kennedy et al. (2010) was obtained through the [Curtin/Figshare record](https://api.figshare.com/v2/articles/31472896). Aleinikoff2007b is the AJS paper, not the other local Aleinikoff2007 copies. Apen2022b is the apatite paper, not the heat-transfer paper.

Deer has a registered book DOI, confirmed by [the publisher](https://pubs.geoscienceworld.org/minersoc/books/book/952/An-Introduction-to-the-Rock-Forming-Minerals). The print ISBN is in the local copyright page; the electronic ISBN is in the DOI metadata. A Crossref bibliographic search and the [Oklahoma Geological Survey catalogue](https://www.ou.edu/ogs/publications/guidebooks/guidebookssearch) did not identify a DOI for the Price guidebook chapter. Its series and paper number identify it.

## Derivations and age labels

Rutile Ti is reproduced exactly by `1e6 * 47.9 / (47.9 + 2*16) = 599499.3742177722 ug/g`. Deer gives ideal TiO2 (p. 393). This verifies the arithmetic and mineral formula, not a measured Ti content of KNW or R632. The notes now state the rounded atomic masses and assumption.

The intended Deer oxide conversion is `oxide wt.% * 10000 * elemental/oxide molar-mass ratio`. The original oxide averages, selected analyses and atomic masses are absent. The stored zircon Si/Zr, titanite Si/Ti and apatite P/Ca numbers could not be reproduced from the available composition tables. Notes now state this limit. No replacement composition was invented.

Stacey and Kramers (1975), Tables 8-9, describe an age-dependent two-stage model, not sample measurements. The second-stage growth law is `R_j(t) = R_j(3700) + p_j * [exp(lambda_j*3700) - exp(lambda_j*t)]`, with ages in Ma, anchors `(11.152, 12.998, 31.23)` and parent/204Pb values `(9.74, 9.74/137.88, 36.84)`. The present-day rounded ratios in Table 8 are `(18.700, 15.628, 38.63)`. The stored triplets lack exact input ages and decay constants. They do not all reproduce this law with modern constants. For example OG1 stores `(16.887460319, 15.46397384563289, 36.53876009569999)`; evaluating the published second-stage anchors at 3465.4 Ma with lambda238 = 1.55125e-4, lambda235 = 9.8485e-4 and lambda232 = 4.9475e-5 per Ma gives about `(11.7700, 13.5553, 31.7405)`. OG1 appears to carry a much younger model assignment. All legacy triplets are retained as unverified; notes now state the intended law and missing inputs.

System-specific weighted means now use the corresponding isotope-age key: 91500, FC1 and OG1 use `207Pb/206Pb_age`; BLR, MKED, Plešovice, Temora-2, R33 and AusZ2 use `206Pb/238U_age`. KHAN uses `upper_intercept_age`. MADUCSB retains `concordia_age`: Apen et al. call its total Pb-U isochron result a concordia age on p. 8.

UPbRPy derives radiogenic ratios that are not stored from `concordia_age` (and from the system ages). ## Convention notes

- `1sd` represents explicitly reported 1s; `2sd` represents explicitly reported absolute 2s or 2σ, including the papers’ mean-age uncertainty convention. Explicit 95% confidence or expanded uncertainty uses `expanded_95`. These conventions are not silently interchanged. `reported_unspecified` is appropriate only where a reported error has no stated coverage. No new percent-to-absolute conversion was needed for the verified values.
- Concentrations are elemental mass fractions in `ug/g`, assigned to isotope-named channels without isotope-abundance multiplication. This includes KNW Pb*, whose Table 5 mass fraction is radiogenic Pb, not measured total Pb. Ratios use `ratio`; ages use `Ma`.
- Radiogenic, common-Pb-corrected ratios use `radiogenic_ratio` and keys ending `*`; measured totals use `total_ratio`; initial/common Pb uses `initial_ratio` and `_i`. Hf/Sr/Nd compositions use `isotope_ratio`. Existing unresolved glass labels were flagged rather than guessed.
